Recipe: Pizza Dip

Ingredients - FOR THE PULL-APART BREAD

  • 16.3 ounce can Grands biscuit dough
  • 8 count ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons parmesan cheese
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

FOR THE PIZZA DIP

  • 8 ounces cream cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup marinara sauce or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese
  • 8 slices pepperoni
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half of dough into a ball.
  3. Combine the melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning in a bowl.
  4. Roll each ball of biscuit dough in the seasoned butter, then arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast iron skillet.
  5. Combine the cream cheese, ricotta cheese and 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning.
  6. Spread the cream cheese mixture in the bottom of the skillet in the middle of the balls of dough.
  7. Top with marinara sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese, Italian seasoning and pepperoni.
  8. Place in the oven and bake for 25-30 minutes.
